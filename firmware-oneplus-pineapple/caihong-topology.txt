Caihong speaker topology

Self-contained ALSA topology source and 12 OEM module configurations from the
validated audio-oem-scene0-v1-20260927 artifact. Rebuild inside caihong-topology/
with alsatplg; relative data paths are intentional.

OEM ACDB SHA256:
4f7be93ab2744d3f694fb4d4af55919128056a5f60067fa25b42793e89c5ecf1

Scene-0 RX/4322 tuning, defaults for the other modules, tagged TDM framing and
the RX/feedback control link are unchanged. The appended measured RE and
rotation records have been removed. The machine driver loads the local
persist calibration and sends RE for indices 0-3 plus rotation 0 before unmute.
There are no invented RE records for channels 5/6.

The stereo frontend and FE/BE mixer use the pinned AudioReach m4 definitions;
the archive includes their expanded output, so no generator checkout or capture
directory is needed at build time. See AUDIO-INVESTIGATION.md for provenance.
